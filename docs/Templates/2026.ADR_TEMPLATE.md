## ADR Quality Checklist
- [ ] Title clearly describes the decision
- [ ] Context provides sufficient background to understand the problem
- [ ] Decision statement is clear and unambiguous
- [ ] Alternatives are reasonably considered with pros/cons
- [ ] Rationale explains why this decision was chosen
- [ ] Status accurately reflects current state
- [ ] Consequences are balanced and realistic
- [ ] Implications consider broader impacts beyond immediate technical concerns
- [ ] Related documents are properly linked
- [ ] Language is clear, concise, and free of jargon where possible
- [ ] Decision focuses on "what" and "why", not just "how"
- [ ] Timeboxed where appropriate (e.g., "we'll revisit this in 6 months")
